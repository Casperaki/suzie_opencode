Life at Rush Grove

A glimpse into daily adventures, routines, and special moments

Every Day is an Adventure

At Rush Grove Nursery, each day is filled with discovery, laughter, and growth. Our unique setting within the historic Rush Grove House and its 3.84 acres of grounds provides endless opportunities for learning through play and exploration.

From morning greetings to goodbye hugs, every moment is carefully nurtured by our dedicated team. Children thrive in our warm, homely environment where their individual interests are celebrated and their curiosity is encouraged.

Activities

Learning Through Play

A rich variety of experiences every day

🎨

Creative Arts

Painting, drawing, clay work, and collage. Children express themselves through various mediums, developing fine motor skills and creativity.

📚

Story Time & Literacy

Daily storytelling, phonics sessions, and access to our cosy book corner. We foster a love of reading from the earliest age.

🔢

Maths & Numbers

Counting games, shape sorting, patterns, and problem-solving activities make maths fun and accessible.

🎵

Music & Movement

Singing, dancing, instruments, and rhythm activities. Music supports language development and coordination.

🌱

Gardening

Planting seeds, watering, harvesting vegetables, and learning about nature's cycles in our garden plots.

🏃

Physical Development

Climbing, balancing, running, and coordination activities both indoors and in our expansive grounds.

Our Food Philosophy

We believe healthy eating habits start early. Our menus are designed by a qualified nutritionist and prepared fresh daily by our experienced cook.

  • ✓ Fresh, seasonal ingredients sourced locally where possible
  • ✓ No added salt or sugar
  • ✓ Accommodates all dietary requirements and allergies
  • ✓ Vegetarian and vegan options daily
  • ✓ Children involved in food preparation and gardening

Starting Journey

Settling In

A gentle transition for children and families

We understand that starting nursery is a big step for both children and parents. Our settling-in process is designed to be gradual and flexible, ensuring every child feels secure and happy.

1

Initial Visit

Family visit to explore the nursery, meet staff, and discuss your child's needs

2

Short Sessions

1-2 hour sessions with parent present, gradually building familiarity

3

Separation Practice

Parent steps out briefly, building confidence for longer separations

4

Full Sessions

Child settles into regular routine with key person support
