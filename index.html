Welcome to Rush Grove Nursery

Nestled within the stunning grounds of Rush Grove House, a magnificent Grade II listed Georgian manor, our nursery offers an unparalleled setting for early years development. Surrounded by 3.84 acres of landscaped gardens, wildlife park, and the serene Mulgrave Pond lake, we provide children with a unique connection to nature while delivering exceptional childcare.

Our philosophy is simple: every child is unique, and we celebrate their individual journey. In our warm, homely environment, children flourish through play, exploration, and discovery. The historic elegance of our setting combines with modern early years expertise to create something truly special.

Our Approach

Nurturing Natural Curiosity

A holistic approach to early years development

📚

EYFS Curriculum

Following the Early Years Foundation Stage framework, we ensure every child develops across all seven areas of learning through carefully planned, engaging activities.

🎨

Play-Based Learning

Children learn best through play. Our approach harnesses natural curiosity, allowing little ones to discover, experiment, and build knowledge joyfully.

🌻

Outdoor Exploration

Our expansive grounds provide endless opportunities for outdoor learning. From nature walks to garden discoveries, children develop a deep connection with the natural world.

💛

Individual Attention

With small group sizes and dedicated key persons, every child receives the attention they need to thrive. We know each child as an individual.

🎭

Creative Expression

Art, music, storytelling, and imaginative play form the heart of our daily activities, fostering creativity and self-expression from the earliest age.

🤝

Partnership with Parents

We believe in working closely with families. Regular communication, parent evenings, and open doors ensure you're always part of your child's journey.

Our Facilities

An Extraordinary Setting

Historic charm meets modern childcare excellence

The Best of Both Worlds

Rush Grove House provides a truly unique nursery environment. Our Georgian manor building has been thoughtfully adapted to create warm, inviting spaces for children while preserving its historic character.

The separate Mews Houses provide intimate, age-appropriate environments for each group, ensuring children feel safe and secure in their own special space.

Our Team

Dedicated Early Years Professionals

Qualified, caring educators who love what they do

At Rush Grove Nursery, our team is our greatest asset. Every member of staff is fully qualified in early years education, with many holding advanced degrees and specialist training.

But beyond qualifications, we look for something harder to measure: a genuine love of working with young children, patience, warmth, and the ability to see the world through a child's eyes.

Our low staff turnover speaks volumes about our supportive environment and shared commitment to excellence. Many of our team have been with us for years, building lasting relationships with children and families.
